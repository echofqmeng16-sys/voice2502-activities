In-Class Activity ② · about 12 minutes

Themes, Textures & Tears
— Bach · Handel · Purcell

Three listening games: catch a fugue theme, tell two textures apart, and map the saddest aria in opera.

👤 First things first — your name

Please enter your name (or student ID). The timer starts as soon as you tap “Start”.

🔎 How it works

  1. Game 1 — Bach, “Little Fugue” in G minor (organ): one theme keeps entering, one voice after another. Tap “Theme entered!” every time you hear it start again. Challenge: catch about 8!
  2. Game 2 — Handel, “Hallelujah”: tap “ALL TOGETHER” when everyone sings the same rhythm at once, and “PASSING IT AROUND” when voices hand the melody to each other like a relay.
  3. Game 3 — Purcell, “Dido’s Lament”: pick up to 3 emotion words, and write one sentence.
  4. Submit: tap the green button at the bottom when you finish.
