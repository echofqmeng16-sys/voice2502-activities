A concerto is a game between one solo violin and the whole orchestra. Your mission: catch the full-orchestra “home theme” every time it comes back.

  1. Piece 1 — Vivaldi, “Spring” (1st movement): the music tells a story (birds, thunderstorm, a barking dog). Each time the whole orchestra plays the big opening theme again, tap “Ritornello is back!”
