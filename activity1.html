In-Class Activity ① · about 8 minutes

Word Painting in Music
— The Weelkes “As Vesta was” Treasure Hunt

An English madrigal from 1601: the composer paints every picture in the poem into the sound itself. Your job: find them all.

👤 First things first — your name

Please enter your name (or student ID). The timer starts as soon as you tap “Start”.

🔎 How it works

  1. Get ready: when the music starts, follow the lyrics below.
  2. Hunt: each time a cue word (highlighted in yellow) is “painted” by the music, tap the matching button as fast as you can ✋
  3. Quiz: after the music ends, answer the 5 matching questions to check you heard how each “painting” is done.
  4. Submit: tap the green button at the bottom when you finish — your instructor collects the results live.

📝 Quiz: how is each word “painted”?

1. How does the music paint “descending” (walking down the hill)?

2. How does it paint “two by two, then three by three”?

3. How does it paint “all alone”?

4. How does the ending “Long live fair Oriana!” sound?

5. What is this technique — “painting words in music” — called?

Tap an option for instant feedback — wrong answers reveal the right one.
