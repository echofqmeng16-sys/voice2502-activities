🎵 The Descending Tetrachord — the Renaissance “code of tears”

AhighGFElow
Dowland builds the entire song on four falling notes (A–G–F–E). Every phrase slides downward. All of Europe was obsessed with this fashionable melancholy at the time — the falling tetrachord was the composers’ agreed “sound of tears.” The tune was later arranged for lute alone as Lachrymae (Latin for “tears”) and became a Europe-wide hit for two centuries.
