🗳️ Which journey was easier to follow?

What you just heard: the sonata form — the “script” of almost every Classical first movement. Mozart fills it with unforgettable melodies; Beethoven fills it with a single four-note cell (short-short-short-LONG). Same script, 20 years apart — one sings, one knocks.
